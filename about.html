About Virginia

Experience, compassion & curiosity.

A holistic and collaborative approach to counselling, grounded in more than 20 years of professional experience.

Professional Background

A life shaped by many places.

Virginia is originally from Uruguay, and has lived in Canada on and off since 1987, having also lived in Africa, the Middle East and Europe since then.

She speaks Spanish fluently, and feels comfortable offering counselling services in that language if needed.

Virginia has been a Registered Social Worker in the province of Alberta since 2005 (Reg. 8049), having graduated with her Bachelor’s in Social Work (BSW) at the University of Calgary that same year.

Since 2012, she holds a Master’s Degree in Social Work (MSW) with a clinical specialization that she also received from the University of Calgary.

She has been in counselling roles since the beginning of her career and has 20 years of counselling experience in Canada.

Therapeutic Approach

A holistic approach to healing and growth.

Virginia practices from a holistic, humanistic, trauma-sensitive, systemic and attachment-based perspective; and has extensive training in Emotionally Focused Therapy.

Most of these approaches are experiential, meaning that they support people’s growth and healing by promoting a safe and healing experience in the counselling room, which people can eventually start to feel and replicate for themselves (self-compassion), in their own environments and with the people around them.

Professional Experience

Experience across many settings.

Throughout her career, she has worked in domestic violence shelters, community mental health clinics, day treatment programs, children and adolescent inpatient mental health units, as well as private practice.

Virginia has worked with individuals, couples and families; and understands the complexity of each human being and of human relationships.

She is comfortable working with people of different ages and different backgrounds.

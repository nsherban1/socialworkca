About Virginia

Experience, compassion & curiosity.

A holistic and collaborative approach to counselling, grounded in more than 20 years of professional experience.

Professional Background

A life shaped by many places.

I am originally from South America although I have lived in Alberta on and off since 1987. I come from a large and complex family who I visit as often as I can. I have had the privilege of living in Africa, the Middle East and Europe and have also had the opportunity to travel quite extensively throughout my life.

I have been blessed with 3 children, now adults, of whom I am extremely proud. They have taught me about life, and helped me grow as a human being in ways that I never imagined they could.

My spare time is spent with family, in nature, and travelling as much as I am able to. I am a great believer that a healthy lifestyle; in which we stay connected with nature and enjoy and appreciate the simple things of life; is helpful in every way. I do my best to live my life that way.

Therapeutic Approach

A holistic approach to healing and growth.

I have been a Registered Social Worker in the province of Alberta since 2005 (Reg. 8049). I studied at the University of Calgary where I obtained both a Bachelor of Social Work (BSW) in 2005, and later a Master Degree in Social Work (MSW) in 2012, with a clinical specialization.

I have been in counselling roles since the beginning of my career and have over 20 years of counselling experience in Canada. I have worked at domestic violence shelters, community mental health clinics, day treatment programs, children and adolescent inpatient mental health units; as well as in private practice; serving individuals, couples and families.

In my practice, I pull from my overall knowledge and experience to find the approach or combination of approaches that is best suited to each specific client and their needs. Although I consider myself to be a “generalist” and welcome every concern or curiosity to my practice, my areas of interest and greater experience are: relational problems of all kinds, trauma, anxiety, depression, managing change, grief, and personal growth.

Professional Experience

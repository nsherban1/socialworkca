Counselling Services

Support that meets you where you are.

I offer virtual counselling services for individuals, couples and families of all ages and backgrounds that reside permanently or temporarily anywhere in Alberta. In person sessions are also offered in NW Calgary intermittently throughout the year. I am happy to offer one-time consultations, or ongoing therapeutic services, depending on the need and the situation.

Virtual sessions are offered through the safe and secure Jane platform, which is also the scheduling system. If applicable, my services are covered by most insurance providers in Alberta. Direct billing is not available at this time.

Philosophy

People are doing the best they can.

I believe people do the best that they can with the resources they have, considering their histories and the specific circumstances of their lives. The great majority of people want to be well and do well for themselves and others. If they are not doing so, is because they do not have the resources or skills to be able to do so.

I see therapy as a process of self discovery. In it, where we can increase our understanding and awareness of ourselves, the impact of our environment; as well as discover strength and resources we may not realize we have. Therapy may also be a place where we can process and change the meaning we attribute to our past; so that we can integrate it in our lives in ways that allow us to move forward and live rich and peaceful lives.

My goal is to support people through that process of self discovery and through periods of transition and loss, in an environment where people feel accepted, safe, seen, heard and cared for. I strive for people to finish the therapy process with increased self awareness, feeling freer from preconceived ideas and limiting beliefs, having more internal peace, and feeling more empowered to take the steps they need to take to feel better about their lives.

Therapy is a collaborative, fluid process where feedback is always welcome. I use different therapeutic modalities to ensure every client gets the approach that is best suited to their individual/family needs.

Your Experience

What to expect.

Counselling in the context of private practice is completely voluntary. If needed, ongoing treatment will likely be discussed collaboratively with you after the first session.

Your counsellor may suggest a specific treatment plan or frequency of sessions; however, it is always up to you to decide whether you will continue after the first session and/or how often you would like to attend.

Counselling can be many different things to different people. In general, people reach out when something in their lives is not going in the way that they would like it to go, when they struggle with mood concerns, addictions, emotional regulation, or when relationships are not working out.

Others reach out for emotional support after a loss, parenting concerns or guidance; and others simply want the support to become the best version of themselves.

Virginia offers help in all those areas, as well as any other curiosity or concern people may present with. She also offers recommendations or referrals to other services if what someone brings to a session is beyond the expertise and support that she is able to provide.

Whatever the initial reason to engage in counselling may be, most people find it helpful and feel that they gain something from it. Often, people leave with more clarity and/or a different perspective on their situation or concern after their first session.

“It is important to feel comfortable with your therapist. The relationship should feel emotionally safe and you should feel seen and heard consistently throughout the process.”

In other words, it is important that the therapist you choose is a good “match” for you. That will make the process more enjoyable and more effective.

The therapist should be open to receiving feedback at any time during the therapeutic process, and be willing to address any concerns openly, with flexibility and without judgement.
